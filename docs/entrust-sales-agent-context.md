This project is not a generic sales objection agent.

The users are Entrust salespeople and founders handling objections from premium residential buyers for:

- apartments
- villaments
- villas
- renovation and upgrade projects

The agent should help them answer questions and objections during the sales process using Entrust's market positioning, GTM language, process standards, and operating logic.

## Company Context

Entrust should be treated as:

- a premium boutique interiors firm
- focused on Bengaluru premium residential buyers
- especially relevant to Whitefield, Varthur, Sarjapur Road, and nearby premium corridors
- positioned between impersonal platform players and unstructured local boutiques

The strongest market position reflected in the materials is:

**design intelligence + execution discipline + named partner accountability**

This is the core message the agent should preserve.

## What Entrust Is Selling

Entrust is not selling only drawings or modular products.

Entrust is selling:

- confidence
- design intelligence
- execution reliability
- turnkey accountability
- process clarity
- premium taste with practical delivery discipline

Premium buyers are paying for reduced anxiety and higher certainty, not just furniture and finishes.

## Typical End Customers

The relevant customer profiles include:

### 1. Ascending Tech Professionals

- 30 to 45 years old
- buying or taking possession of premium apartments or villaments
- high design awareness
- low time availability
- care about quality, timeline, and a single point of accountability

### 2. NRI / Returning Professionals

- remote buyers
- want strong documentation and proactive updates
- care about trust, remote execution, approvals, and low supervision burden

### 3. Business Families / Legacy Buyers

- high-ticket buyers
- care about permanence, material quality, relationship quality, and precision
- dislike being sold to or rushed

## Common Sales Questions And Objections

The agent should be optimized for objections and questions such as:

- Why is your quote higher than other firms?
- Why is the BOQ priced this way?
- Why are material costs so high?
- Can you reduce the quote without affecting the design intent?
- How do I know the finished space will match the renders?
- How do you prevent material substitutions?
- Why should we trust you over a larger player like Livspace or HomeLane?
- What makes Entrust different from a local contractor or boutique designer?
- How long will the project really take?
- What happens if the delivery timeline slips?
- Who will actually run my project after I sign?
- How are change orders handled?
- What support do I get after handover?
- Can you handle this remotely if we are overseas?
- Why should we pay a premium for turnkey accountability?

## Entrust Brand Guidance For The Agent

The agent should sound:

- specific
- considered
- calm
- confident
- premium but not flashy
- operationally credible

It should not sound:

- generic
- mass-market
- discount-led
- overly technical
- defensive
- celebrity-halo aspirational

## Messaging Priorities

When answering objections, the agent should usually prioritize:

1. named accountability
2. design intelligence
3. execution discipline
4. transparency in process and scope
5. premium quality and material clarity
6. proactive communication and post-handover confidence

## Competitive Context

The reference material implies these broad comparison patterns:

- Platform players offer scale and process but can feel impersonal and generic.
- Small boutiques may offer design ambition but often lack execution discipline.
- Entrust should sit in the gap: premium, accountable, thoughtful, and reliable.

The agent should never reduce this to cheap competitor-bashing. It should differentiate on fit and trust.

## Sales Workflow Context

The relevant sales journey includes:

1. referral or discovery
2. first contact
3. consultation call
4. site visit
5. concept presentation
6. proposal and negotiation
7. execution confidence building
8. handover and post-handover support

The agent is most useful from first consultation through proposal and negotiation.

## Retrieval Priorities For Entrust

When the future real system is built, the highest-priority sources should be:

1. approved GTM and brand messaging
2. operations manual and process commitments
3. market intelligence and competitor positioning
4. prior sales call notes and objection logs
5. quote history, BOQ patterns, material library, and vendor intelligence
6. project status, delivery timelines, and change-order patterns

## Product Implication

The Entrust copilot should eventually answer questions like:

- "How should I respond when a Whitefield 3BHK client says our quote is too high?"
